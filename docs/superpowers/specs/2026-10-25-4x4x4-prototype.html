Cube only, with tap to select and tap again to place

Drag to rotate. Tap a number on the left to pick a layer; the other layers fade. Tap one of that layer's small cubes to select it; it turns see-through in the player's colour. Tap it again to place, or tap another cube to move the selection. Tapping empty space or another layer number cancels. "Demo position" sets up a space diagonal for X: pick layer 4, then tap its corner cube closest to the bottom of the screen twice.
